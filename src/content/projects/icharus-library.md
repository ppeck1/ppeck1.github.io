## What it is

Not a reading list, encyclopedia, or curriculum. A navigational cognition instrument—a decision geometry atlas for asking: "When humans faced X problem across time, what craft solutions recurred?"

Organized under three invariant roots:

- **Provision**: sustaining bodies (food, shelter, heat, medicine)
- **Protection**: safety and repairable trust (warning, repair, law, containment)
- **Meaning**: memory and purpose (teaching, stories, ritual, coherence)

## How it works

Navigate by asking rotational questions: "How does this function appear in other times/places? What domain-spanning problems reoccur?"

Unlike timeline-based histories, the atlas extracts invariants across civilizations without claiming universal truth. Each node is a stable human question expressed as a craft interface. Nodes map to functions within domains within roots.
