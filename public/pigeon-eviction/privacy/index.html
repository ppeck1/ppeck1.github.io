Pigeon Eviction privacy policy

Pigeon Eviction · Effective November 15, 2026

Pigeon Eviction is an offline game developed by Paul Peck. This policy explains how the game handles information on your device and how Android backup may preserve your progress.

Offline play and data collection

The game has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, in-app purchases or developer-operated crash reporting. Playing does not require an internet connection. The game does not collect, sell or transmit personal data to its developer or advertisers. It does not ask for your name or email address, and does not access your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

What is saved on your device

The game stores your current match and selected birds, results, collectible bird collection, and sound and motion settings in its private app storage. These records let you resume a match, keep earned birds and retain your preferences. They are not linked to an account or sent to a developer server.

Android backup and device transfer

Android may back up this save to your backup provider or copy it during device-to-device transfer, depending on your device, system settings and backup provider. The game permits backup only of its save file. The developer does not receive or control these backups. Their protection, retention and removal are governed by your device and backup provider settings and policies.

Retention, deletion and resetting progress

The local save remains until app storage is cleared or the app is removed. To reset the game, use Clear storage or Clear data for Pigeon Eviction in Android Settings; this removes local matches, settings and the bird collection. Uninstalling normally removes the local save, but Android may restore a previous backup after reinstalling or setting up another device. Clearing local storage alone does not guarantee removal of backup copies; manage those through your device or backup provider. There is no game account or developer-held gameplay profile to delete.

Children

The same offline data handling applies to every player. The game does not request personal information from children or provide messaging or user-generated content features.

Stores, Android and external links

Android, your app store and your backup provider may process information under their own policies, including installation, purchase or system diagnostic information. The support button opens an email app or browser. It does not send your local game save or send a message automatically. Email is handled by your email provider under its own policies.

Privacy questions

Paul Peck is the developer of Pigeon Eviction. For support or privacy questions, email paultpeck@gmail.com. If you send an email, Paul receives your email address and the information you choose to include, and uses that correspondence to answer and resolve your inquiry. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to resolve the inquiry or meet applicable obligations. You can request its deletion at the same address. Support messages are separate from the game save; include only information needed for your inquiry.

Policy updates

Changes to the game's data handling will be reflected in an updated privacy policy and app release. The effective date identifies this policy version.
