Socrates

Greek philosopher whose injunction to 'know thyself' and care for the soul set the agenda for Western reflection on the self, though he authored no positions in this slice directly.

Identity and orientation

Socrates left no writings. His historical setting is classical Athens after war, democratic instability, sophistic education, and intense public argument about virtue and civic judgment.

Epistemic caution
Later busts and paintings are reception images, not verified likenesses. Distinguish historical Socrates from Platonic literary construction.

Why this thinker reasoned this way

The Socratic method tests whether confident language survives examination. The recurring pressure is ethical and epistemic: a city can coordinate around words it has not actually clarified.

Transmission note

The evidence problem is constitutive. Plato, Xenophon, Aristophanes, and later authors present different Socrates figures. No single dialogue can be treated as a transcript.
