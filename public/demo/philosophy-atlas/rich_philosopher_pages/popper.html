Identity and orientation

Karl Popper worked in twentieth-century philosophy of science and political thought against the background of European crisis, exile, and debates about scientific rationality.

Epistemic caution
Use rights-verified photographs only. Do not claim that one falsifying observation mechanically settles every scientific dispute.

Why this thinker reasoned this way

Falsifiability is a demarcation proposal and methodological pressure: theories should expose themselves to possible failure rather than merely accumulate confirming instances.

The Skeptical Modes

Both treat inquiry as the ongoing exposure of unsupported claims rather than the attainment of final certainty, though Popper keeps a robust notion of objective progress the skeptic withholds.

opposition · interpretive

Certainty in the Cogito

Foundationalist certainty versus fallibilist error-elimination: Descartes seeks an indubitable base; Popper denies any is needed or available and makes refutation the engine of knowledge.
