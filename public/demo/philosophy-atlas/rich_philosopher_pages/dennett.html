Identity and orientation

Daniel Dennett worked in late twentieth- and early twenty-first-century philosophy of mind alongside cognitive science, artificial intelligence, biology, and public debate.

Epistemic caution
Do not present a metaphor as a complete empirical model, or assume publicly visible photographs are reusable.

Why this thinker reasoned this way

His multiple-drafts approach resists a single inner Cartesian theater. Explanatory pressure should move toward distributed processes rather than a hidden central witness.
