Identity and orientation

The Laozi or Daodejing is a layered classical Chinese text conventionally associated with Laozi. Traditional biography and textual history do not map neatly onto a single historically verified author.

Epistemic caution
Traditional Laozi images are iconographic reception. Do not flatten wuwei into passivity or Dao into a Western metaphysical substance.

Why this thinker reasoned this way

Its recurring pressure is over-coercion: rigid action, naming, and control can destabilize what they attempt to master. Reversal and non-coercive action are governance and life diagnostics.
