What is knowledge?

position_answers_question

critique · interpretive

Knowledge Through Examined Ignorance

Gettier targets the justified-true-belief analysis often traced to Plato's Socrates, showing justification plus truth can still fall short of knowledge.

opposition · interpretive

Knowledge as Valid Cognition (Pramana)

Dignaga grounds knowledge in the reliable operation of cognitive instruments (perception, inference); Gettier shows that on a justification-based analysis, reliable-seeming justification can still misfire — two different diagnoses of when cognition counts as knowing.
