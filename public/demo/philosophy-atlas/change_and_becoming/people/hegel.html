Core problems and positions

Answers: Is reality fundamentally process?

Becoming as Dialectical Development

Pure being and pure nothing are empty and pass into each other; their truth is becoming. Reality and thought develop dialectically through the generation and resolution of contradictions, so change is the self-movement of the concept, not mere external alteration.

Historical setting

Hegel’s becoming arises from German Idealism’s attempt to explain thought, freedom, history, and reality after Kant’s critique of dogmatic metaphysics. The French Revolution and modern social upheaval also matter: change is no longer merely a natural puzzle but a historical and institutional fact demanding conceptual account.

Becoming and dialectical development

In the Logic, pure being collapses into indeterminacy and is indistinguishable from nothing; becoming names their unstable unity. Dialectic is not a mechanical three-step slogan. It is the immanent movement by which a concept’s own one-sidedness generates tension, negation, preservation, and transformation into a richer determination.

Cautions and misreadings

The formula thesis-antithesis-synthesis is a later simplification and often misleads. Hegel is better represented as tracing immanent contradictions and determinate negation. In lineages, distinguish his documented debts to Kant, Fichte, Schelling, Aristotle, and Spinoza from loose parallels with Daoist reversal or Buddhist emptiness.

Historical setting

G. W. F. Hegel wrote after the French Revolution and during the reorganization of European political and intellectual institutions. His system treats categories, history, and social forms as dynamically related.

Why this thinker reasoned this way

Contradictions and inadequacies are not decorative drama. A form can generate pressures that expose its limits and require a richer determination.

Texts and transmission

The Phenomenology, Science of Logic, Encyclopaedia, lectures, and editorial histories should be separated. Later summaries often substitute a formula for textual engagement.
