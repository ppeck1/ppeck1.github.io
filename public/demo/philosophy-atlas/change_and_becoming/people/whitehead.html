Answers: Is reality fundamentally process?

Reality as Process of Actual Occasions

The basic units of reality are not enduring substances but momentary 'actual occasions' of experience that arise, integrate their world, and perish. Enduring objects are societies of such occasions; process is fundamental, permanence derivative.

Historical setting

Whitehead’s process metaphysics grows from a mathematician-philosopher’s encounter with modern science. After work on logic and mathematics, he turns to the metaphysical implications of relativity, organism, event, and relation. The target is the inherited picture of enduring substances with accidental relations.

Actual occasions and process

Whitehead treats actuality as composed of actual occasions: momentary processes of prehension, integration, and satisfaction. Objects persist as societies or ordered patterns of occasions. Change is not something added to being; being is constituted by becoming.

Cautions and uses

Whitehead has a technical vocabulary and a demanding systematic architecture. A useful atlas representation should show event, relation, prehension, society, and creativity without reducing the system to the slogan that everything is connected.

Historical setting

Alfred North Whitehead moved from mathematics and logic into a mature process metaphysics shaped by science, philosophy, and teaching in Britain and the United States.

Why this thinker reasoned this way

His basic pressure is eventful reality: static substance language can conceal becoming, relation, and the organization of experience into occasions.

Texts and transmission

Process and Reality is textually difficult and historically situated within lectures and revisions. Later process-theology receptions are important but not identical to Whitehead’s own project.
