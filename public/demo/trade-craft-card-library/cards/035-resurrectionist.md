    ## Rewritten card

    Resurrectionist sits where technique and legitimacy touch. Stealing corpses for anatomy schools where legal supply failed. Morally grim, historically real, and tied to medical training.

In the Industrial Age, work like this cannot be separated cleanly from belief, law, memory, rank, or public trust. The craft mattered because it produced an outcome other people were willing to recognize as valid. Why it mattered: Science created a corpse market.

The strange edge is this: Demand for knowledge produced nocturnal crime. The material act was only half the job. The other half was making the act socially legible.
