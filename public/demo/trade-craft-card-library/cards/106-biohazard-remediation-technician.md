    ## Rewritten card

    This card is best read as a piece of infrastructure, not as a quaint job title. Biohazard remediation technician handled the matter, hours, or spaces that more prestigious systems preferred not to face directly. Cleaning and decontaminating spaces after traumatic events, contamination, or unattended death combines safety protocol with difficult human context.

The work was necessary because settlements, markets, armies, workshops, and households produce residue. Why it matters: Some essential work begins after everyone else leaves.

This is hidden labor. It becomes visible only when it fails, when it smells, when it blocks movement, when disease spreads, or when a cleaner system has not yet been built. Modern echo: Infrastructure includes emotional as well as material residue.
