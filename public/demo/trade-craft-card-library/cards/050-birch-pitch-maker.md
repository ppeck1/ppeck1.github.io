    Birch pitch maker was a material-intelligence role. Heating bark under oxygen-poor conditions produces a sticky tar useful for hafting blades, sealing containers, and repairing tools.

The worker had to read behavior in heat, fracture, grain, airflow, moisture, season, wear, or chemical change. Why it feels strange: A chemical supply chain existed before pottery or metal.
