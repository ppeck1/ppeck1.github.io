    ## Rewritten card

    Ritual technologist sits where technique and legitimacy touch. Designing communal rites using projection, sensors, archives, synthetic voices, and symbolic interfaces.

In the Speculative Future, work like this cannot be separated cleanly from belief, law, memory, rank, or public trust. The craft mattered because it produced an outcome other people were willing to recognize as valid. Why it matters: Meaning-making follows tools.

The strange edge is this: Priest, stage tech, and UX merge. The material act was only half the job. The other half was making the act socially legible.
